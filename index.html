How to use

This sign-up page is managed manually and is not connected to CourtReserve.

  1. Tap Join on the session you want to play, then enter your name.
  2. If the court is booked under your name, add the players to the booking in CourtReserve.
  3. If you can’t make it, transfer the CourtReserve booking to another player. Then tap Edit here and update the “Booked under” name.
  4. Changed your mind? Tap Leave on the session to remove your name.
